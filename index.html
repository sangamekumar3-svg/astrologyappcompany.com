Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions founders ask before developing an astrology consultation application.

How does per-minute consultation billing work? +
When a user initiates an audio call, video call, or chat with an astrologer, our real-time websocket server verifies their wallet balance. The system automatically debits the user's wallet second-by-second at the astrologer's set rate (e.g., ₹25/min). If the balance runs out, the call automatically disconnects.
How accurate are the Kundli and Horoscope calculations? +
We integrate the industry-standard Swiss Ephemeris astronomical library, which powers leading software like AstroSage. It calculates precise planetary longitudes, ascendants, and dashas accurate to fractions of a second of arc.
Why partner with an astrology app development company in India? +
India is the epicentre of Vedic astrology logic and modern mobile engineering. Choosing an astrology app development company in India provides direct access to developers who understand Indian spiritual culture, panchang systems, UPI wallet flows, and cost-effective turnaround compared to western development agencies.
Can you build custom destiny solution mobile apps with live consultations? +
Yes. We engineer complete destiny solution mobile apps featuring instant birth chart generation, numerology match scores, tarot card spreads, and low-latency audio/video calling. We tailor the entire destiny and life-guidance architecture to match your branding.
Will I own the complete source code and database? +
Yes, 100%. Upon project completion and final milestone sign-off, Sunra Soft Tech transfers the full intellectual property rights, GitHub repositories, and server credentials directly to your organization.
Can astrologers withdraw their earnings automatically? +
Yes. Astrologers have a dedicated wallet dashboard showing consultation minutes, gross earnings, and platform commission deductions. They can request payouts via UPI or bank transfer directly through the astrologer app.
